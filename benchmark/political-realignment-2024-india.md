# Harness Research Benchmark: India 2024 election

## Research question

Was India's 2024 general-election result a one-off correction or evidence of a durable political realignment?

Assess changes in vote share, coalition strategy, regional outcomes, major voter blocs, and governing constraints through 11 October 2026. Separate verified facts from interpretation; prioritize Election Commission data, party documents, parliamentary records, government publications, and named expert analysis. Identify the strongest case for each conclusion and where the evidence is genuinely inconclusive.

## Common operating rules

- **Model:** `gpt-6.1-sol`.
- **Reasoning effort:** `medium`.
- **Cutoff for new evidence:** 11 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata.
- **Research mode:** autonomous web research is allowed; cite direct URLs for every material factual claim.
- **Fairness:** do not provide one harness a private source pack, a human-written outline, or an extra model pass.
- **Deliverable:** a 1,500–2,500 word decision memo with an executive conclusion, competing interpretations, a compact evidence table, source links, and a section explicitly labelled `Uncertainties and limits`.
- **Evaluation:** factual correctness, source quality and traceability, treatment of competing evidence, analytical clarity, completeness, and wall-clock time. Preserve raw transcripts/logs where the harness exposes them.

## What this benchmark is testing

This is deliberately a non-technical, politically sensitive research task. A good result must retrieve current primary evidence, distinguish data from commentary, resist treating a close electoral result as proof of a single narrative, and make uncertainty legible.
