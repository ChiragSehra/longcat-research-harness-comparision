# ResearchSpec Critique

**Verdict: structurally strong, but revise before execution.** The plan covers every explicit user requirement, has five user-facing main sections and eight substantive H3s, and assigns empirical ownership sensibly. Its principal weaknesses are source readiness, excessive scope within the word budget, and insufficient protection against selecting governing episodes that favor one interpretation.

The independent check attempted seven searches; all failed with HTTP 429. Browsing recovered useful Lokniti, PRS, Journal of Democracy, and government budget pages, but no usable ECI returns or complete election census. Consequently, this review cannot certify electoral coverage through the cutoff or establish additional missing election outcomes. That limitation should remain explicit.

## Actionable findings

1. **Make source recovery a concrete dependency, not another general instruction.** Every H3 has Required Entities / Cases and Source Leads, but most electoral and coalition leads are collection pages. S2.1–S2.2 cannot yet execute their central comparisons from the fetched evidence. Require a document-level source manifest before calculations, including unresolved entries. A successfully fetched ECI page containing only its title does not satisfy the recovery gate.

2. **Retain the full election census in working notes, but bound the memo’s case selection.** S2.2 combines national geographic decomposition, four concurrent assembly comparisons, and every subsequent assembly election. Specify a reproducible selection rule: largest contributions to national change, comparable subsequent tests, and at least one strong contrary case. Otherwise the 800-word electoral allocation invites selective anecdotes or superficial coverage.

3. **Separate administrative adaptation, legislative control, and constitutional arithmetic.** S3.2 lists enough episodes to overwhelm its allocation. Select cases only after checking their evidentiary value: an ordinary-law passage, a constitutional proposal, and an administrative reversal or documented bargaining concession. Do not treat these as interchangeable measures of coalition constraint.

4. **Tighten the bargaining handoff.** S3.1 should establish the demand, actor, timing, and evidence connecting a concession to bargaining. S3.2 should own its legislative or fiscal delivery. Create one shared episode record so both researchers do not independently narrate Andhra Pradesh/Bihar concessions.

5. **Reserve space explicitly for the evidence table.** The section allocations total approximately 2,100 words without stating where the six-to-eight-row table fits. Assign its words to a section or reserve a separate allowance. Keep methodological inventories out of the memo.

## Missing Named Methods and Source Leads

- **HP4 — Systematic parliamentary continuity comparison → S3.2.** Add the named PRS assessments *Functioning of the 17th Lok Sabha*, *Parliament in Inaugural and Budget Session 2024*, and session assessments through *Monsoon Session 2026*. Use them to select consequential episodes and identify contrary evidence, then corroborate with parliamentary records. [Source lead](https://prsindia.org/parliamenttrack/vital-stats). These are specific discovery targets, not substitutes for divisions or proceedings.

- **HP5 — Budget implementation records → S3.2, with the bargaining linkage supplied by S3.1.** The government’s [2025–26 budget collection](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/budget2025-26/) lists *Implementation of Budget Announcements 2024–2025*; the [2026–27 collection](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/) lists the corresponding 2025–26 implementation document. Add these named documents alongside allocations and release records. Their existence is verified; their contents have not yet been inspected, and they cannot independently prove ally causation.

Existing HP1–HP3 should remain. The fetched [Vaishnav essay page](https://www.journalofdemocracy.org/articles/the-rise-of-indias-second-republic/) supports the attributed continuity argument, but its abstract cannot substitute for examining the full reasoning. No additional named analytical method is justified by this check; adding adjacent-domain techniques would inflate the plan.

## Prioritized revision checklist

1. Recover direct ECI returns and reconcile the complete election census before selecting persistence cases.
2. Absorb HP4–HP5 into their target H3s, or explicitly reject each with an evidence-based reason; retrieve their underlying documents.
3. Define a balanced governing-episode selection rule and the S3.1/S3.2 handoff.
4. Set a diagnostic regional-case selection rule and allocate words to the evidence table.
5. Replace generic source leads with document-level URLs as recovery succeeds; retain explicit access and verification limits where it fails.

These are execution revisions. The outline does not need rewriting, additional H3s, or a predetermined verdict.
