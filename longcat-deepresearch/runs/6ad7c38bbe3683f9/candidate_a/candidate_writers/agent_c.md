# ReportSpec

### Global Research Boundaries
- Produce a 1,500–2,500 word decision memo answering whether India's 2024 general-election result was a one-off correction or evidence of durable political realignment. Cover evidence available through 11 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata; exclude later developments and distinguish event dates from publication dates.
- Use 2014 and 2019 as electoral baselines, 2024 as the central event, and subsequent elections and governing conduct as persistence tests. Define realignment separately for voter loyalties, coalition organization, regional competition, and governing power; these need not move together.
- Prioritize Election Commission of India (ECI) final statistical reports and constituency returns, dated party documents, parliamentary records, and government publications. Use methodologically documented surveys for voter-bloc claims and attributed, named expert analysis for interpretation.
- Cite a direct document or result-page URL for every material factual claim. Distinguish verified fact, survey estimate, researcher calculation, attributed interpretation, and unresolved claim. Party documents establish stated positions, not voter motives or implementation.
- Planning searches were attempted with complementary electoral, voter-behaviour, and governing-constraint queries, but all searches failed with HTTP 429. Accessible pages below establish source availability, not election outcomes or causal conclusions. The attempted June 2024 ECI result URL returned 404; locate a working official archive rather than cite it. No outcome should be inferred from homepage snippets.
- Budget approximately 150 words for the executive conclusion, 350 for competing interpretations, 850 for electoral evidence, 450 for coalition and governing constraints, and 200 for uncertainties. Integrate one compact evidence table; avoid a separate encyclopedic history.
