# ReportSpec

### Global Research Boundaries
- Produce a 1,500–2,500 word decision memo answering whether India's 2024 general-election result was a one-off correction or evidence of durable political realignment. Include an executive conclusion, competing interpretations, one compact evidence table, direct source links, and a section explicitly labelled “Uncertainties and limits.”
- Cut off events and evidence at 11 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata. Verify event dates and publication dates separately; exclude later developments and retrospective knowledge. Use 2014 and 2019 as national baselines, with earlier history only where needed to define realignment.
- Distinguish three outcomes: temporary electoral correction; durable change in voter alignment or party competition; durable coalition dependence without equivalent voter realignment. These can coexist across regions.
- Prioritize ECI final statistical reports and constituency returns, party documents, parliamentary proceedings and divisions, and government publications. Use Lokniti-CSDS surveys for voter behaviour and named expert analysis for interpretation. Party statements establish stated positions, not the truth of allegations; government publicity establishes announcements, not implementation or political causation.
- Every material factual claim in the memo needs a direct URL to the supporting document or page. Source leads below are retrieval starting points, not final citations. Record derived calculations, denominators, alliance membership, and source dates.
- Planning attempted more than three complementary searches covering results, voter blocs, expert interpretations, subsequent elections, and coalition constraints; all search calls returned HTTP 429. Direct fetching located ECI portals, Lokniti national/state study indexes, parliamentary resources, party material, budget documents, and specific legislative leads. Accordingly, the plan claims no verified census of subsequent results or substantive expert positions. Complete those searches and retrieve underlying documents before drafting.
- Allocate roughly 180 words to the executive conclusion, 760 to electoral and social evidence, 520 to coalitions and governing constraints, 420 to competing interpretations, and 220 to uncertainties. Target approximately 2,100 words including the evidence table.

#### What to Cover

State which interpretation has the strongest support, distinguish electoral from institutional durability, and identify the most important contrary evidence. Define what would count as realignment rather than equating a seat loss with structural change.
#### Research Questions
- Do changes in votes, voter blocs, alliance coordination, and governing behaviour point in the same direction?
- Which findings persist beyond June 2024, and which reverse or remain untested through the cutoff?
- Is a national binary judgment defensible, or does a mixed regional and institutional conclusion better fit the evidence?
#### Required Entities / Cases
- One-off correction, durable voter/party-system realignment, and durable coalition dependence.
- BJP versus NDA; Congress versus INDIA; national versus regional change.
- Findings and counterevidence from S2–S4.

#### Presentation

## S2｜Electoral and voter evidence

### S2.1｜National votes, seats, and the conversion mechanism
#### What to Cover
Compare national party and coalition performance in 2014, 2019, and 2024. Explain how vote distribution, constituency competition, and alliances translated votes into seats. Separate a change in popular support from a change in opposition coordination or electoral efficiency.
#### Research Questions
- What are the final BJP, Congress, and major-party vote shares and seat totals in each election, and their percentage-point and seat changes?
- How do NDA and opposition aggregates change when calculated using contemporaneous alliance membership rather than today's membership?
- How much of the 2019–2024 seat change is concentrated in close contests or particular states? Does geographic redistribution help explain modest national vote movement alongside larger seat movement?
- How do contested-seat shares, turnout, uncontested constituencies, NOTA, and different vote denominators affect comparisons?
#### Required Entities / Cases
- ECI general-election statistical reports for 2014, 2019, and 2024; constituency-level votes and margins.
- BJP, Congress, NDA, INDIA, and nonaligned parties; the 543-seat elected House and majority threshold.
- SP, TDP, JD(U), AITC, DMK, BSP, AAP, and the separate Shiv Sena and NCP factions where relevant to aggregation.
#### Source Leads
- https://www.eci.gov.in/statistical-reports — retrieve final party, state, turnout, and constituency tables.
- https://www.eci.gov.in/statistical-report/statistical-reports — alternate official report entry point; underlying tables require retrieval.
- https://prsindia.org/parliamenttrack/vital-stats — locate profiles of the 17th and 18th Lok Sabha for cross-checking representation, not replacing ECI returns.
#### Presentation
Own the memo's single compact evidence table. Use approximately six to eight rows covering national votes/seats, regional concentration, subsequent elections, social blocs, alliance persistence, and governing constraints. Columns: verified observation/date; implication for correction; implication for realignment; limitation; direct source.
