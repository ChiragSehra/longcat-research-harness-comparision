# ReportSpec

## Research mandate and evidence rules

Produce an English **1,500–2,500 word decision memo** answering: **Was India's 2024 general-election result a one-off correction or evidence of a durable political realignment?** Assess vote shares, coalition strategy, regional outcomes, major voter blocs, and governing constraints through **11 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata**. Include an executive conclusion, competing interpretations, one compact evidence table, direct source links, and a section explicitly labelled **“Uncertainties and limits.”** Do not preselect the verdict.

Distinguish four propositions: temporary electoral correction; persistent voter-bloc realignment; durable regional party-system change; and enduring coalition constraints on governing. They can coexist. Define durability through repeated, comparable observations rather than seat losses alone. Use 2014 and 2019 as national baselines, 2024 as the central election, and subsequent completed elections and governing conduct as persistence tests.

Prioritize final Election Commission of India (ECI) returns, dated party documents, parliamentary records, and government publications. Use Lokniti-CSDS surveys for individual voting patterns and signed, dated expert analysis for interpretation. Clearly distinguish verified facts, survey estimates, researcher calculations, party claims, attributed interpretation, and unresolved claims. Cite a **direct supporting URL for every material factual claim**; collection pages below are discovery leads unless their own contents support the claim. Record event and publication dates separately and exclude evidence published after the cutoff.

Judge-stage searches independently attempted national results, subsequent elections, constitutional constraints, and expert interpretations; all four returned HTTP 429. Fetches confirmed survey indexes and specific PRS legislative leads, but did not yield an official election-result census or signed expert arguments. Consequently, the election inventory below is a **verification checklist**, not a verified list of outcomes. Do not carry candidates' unsupported 2025–2026 claims into the memo. Recover document-level evidence before drafting; disclose material remaining gaps.

Target approximately 2,100 words: executive conclusion 180; electoral and voter evidence 800; coalition and governing evidence 450; competing interpretations 450; uncertainties 220, including the evidence table. Keep research inventories in working notes rather than adding a separate deliverable.

## S2｜Electoral change and persistence

### S2.1｜National votes, seats, and electoral conversion
#### What to Cover
Compare party votes, vote shares, seats won, seats contested, and turnout in 2014, 2019, and 2024. Explain first-past-the-post seat conversion through geographic distribution, close contests, and opposition coordination. Separate popular-support changes from alliance reclassification.
#### Research Questions
- What do final ECI tables establish for BJP, Congress, and the major regional parties? What are changes in percentage points and seats?
- How do coalition totals differ using election-date membership and a consistently defined party grouping? Do not retrospectively call the 2014 or 2019 opposition INDIA.
- Using constituency-level swing and margin analysis, where did small vote changes produce substantial seat changes?
- What do contested-seat shares reveal that national shares conceal? How are valid-vote denominators, NOTA, uncontested seats, and rounding handled?
- Can coordination effects be supported by contest patterns and surveys without assuming perfect vote transfer in a hypothetical counterfactual?
#### Required Entities / Cases
- BJP, Congress, SP, TMC/AITC, DMK, TDP, JD(U), BSP, AAP, and material nonaligned parties.
- Separate Shiv Sena and NCP factions using official party identifiers.
- Final national, state, and constituency returns; election-specific alliance membership.
#### Source Leads

- https://results.eci.gov.in/
- https://prsindia.org/parliamenttrack/vital-stats — representation cross-checks, not a substitute for ECI returns.

Build the memo's **single compact evidence table**, with six to eight decisive rows spanning all empirical units. Columns: indicator/date; verified observation; implication for correction; implication for realignment; principal limitation; direct source. Label derived totals and calculations.
