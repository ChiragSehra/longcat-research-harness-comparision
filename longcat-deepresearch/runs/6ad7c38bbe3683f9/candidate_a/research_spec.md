# ReportSpec

## Research mandate and shared execution rules

Produce an English **1,500–2,500 word decision memo** answering: **Was India's 2024 general-election result a one-off correction or evidence of a durable political realignment?** Assess vote shares, coalition strategy, regional outcomes, major voter blocs, and governing constraints through **11 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata**. Include an executive conclusion, competing interpretations, a compact evidence table, direct source links, and a section explicitly labelled **“Uncertainties and limits.”** Do not preselect the verdict.

Distinguish four propositions: temporary electoral correction; persistent voter-bloc realignment; durable regional party-system change; and enduring coalition constraints. BJP dominance, regional opposition gains, and reduced parliamentary autonomy can coexist. For each proposition, record the observation, comparator, confounders, strongest contrary evidence, and confidence. Assembly outcomes, seat changes, or coalition formation alone cannot establish national voter realignment.

Use 2014 and 2019 national elections as baselines, 2024 as the central observation, and verified subsequent elections and governing conduct as persistence tests. Prioritize final ECI returns, original party documents, parliamentary records, and government publications. Use Lokniti-CSDS for individual voting estimates and named, signed expert analysis for interpretation. Separate verified facts, survey estimates, researcher calculations, party claims, attributed interpretation, and unresolved claims. Every material factual claim in the memo requires a direct supporting URL. Record event and publication dates; exclude publications after the cutoff.

**Document recovery dependency:** before calculations or case selection, create a document-level source manifest in working notes. Each entry must identify the required election, survey, agreement, or governing episode; document title; issuer; direct URL; publication/event dates; accessible substantive content; relevant table/page; and verification status. Record unresolved entries, attempted URLs, and any authoritative replacement. A title-only fetched page does not satisfy this gate. Collection pages below are discovery routes; replace them with direct supporting documents as recovery succeeds. Source Leads remain unverified until fetched by the Researcher, including leads described as retrieved in the Critic's check. Earlier searches encountered HTTP 429 and a June 2024 results archive returned 404; do not invent replacement paths. Independent calculations may proceed where inputs are recovered, but missing coverage must remain visible and qualify synthesis.

**Ownership and handoffs:** S2.1 owns electoral arithmetic and measured coordination effects; S2.2 geography, election census, and comparable persistence tests; S2.3 survey evidence; S3.1 agreements, alliance survival, and bargaining linkage; S3.2 legislative, administrative, and fiscal delivery. Maintain one shared record per governing episode: demand and actor; dated supporting documents; decision and delivery; institutional mechanism; contrary explanation; confidence. S3.1 supplies the demand–concession linkage and S3.2 verifies delivery; neither independently duplicates the narrative. S1 and S4 synthesize existing findings.

**Memo budget:** target approximately 2,100 words: executive conclusion 180; electoral and voter evidence 650; coalition and governing evidence 400; competing interpretations 400; evidence table 250; uncertainties 220. The table belongs within electoral evidence and contains six to eight decisive rows: indicator/date; verified observation; implication for correction; implication for realignment; principal limitation; direct source. Label calculations and derived alliance totals. Keep manifests, full inventories, and calculations in working notes; never create a separate Rowbook.
