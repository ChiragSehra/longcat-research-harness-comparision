### S2.3｜Major voter blocs and explanations of vote choice

**The recovered survey evidence is insufficient to establish durable voter-bloc realignment.** Lokniti-CSDS lists unweighted post-poll samples of 22,295 respondents across 26 states or Union Territories and 306 constituencies in 2014; 24,236 across 26 and 208 in 2019; and 19,663 across 23 and 191 in 2024. Its catalogue distinguishes these samples from weighted reports and lists questionnaires and methodological notes. The counts verify reported coverage, but geographic coverage differs between elections. [Lokniti national-election-study catalogue](https://www.lokniti.org/national-election-studies)

Weighted tables, instruments and methodological notes remain unresolved, preventing defensible numerical comparisons for SC/Dalit, ST/Adivasi, OBC, upper-caste, Muslim, female, young, poorer and rural voters, including Uttar Pradesh’s Yadav/non-Yadav OBC and Jatav/other Dalit contrasts. Comparisons require subgroup denominators, weighting details and consistent geographic coverage and category definitions; national sample totals cannot establish subgroup precision, and BSP’s decline alone cannot identify caste switching. Explanations involving jobs, prices, farming, welfare, leadership, reservation and constitutional protection likewise require original questions and response tables: campaign promises and economic indicators do not establish voting motives.

No later comparable subgroup study is verified in the recovered evidence, leaving persistence through 11 October 2026 unresolved. Even comparable repeated cross-sections would measure changes in group-level support rather than track individual voters’ switching.
